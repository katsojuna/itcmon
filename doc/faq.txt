Possible issues:

Programs just end without displaying ANYTHING at all as soon as started:

  This could be a cygwin1.dll issue.  If you have other cygwin compiled
  programs running from a different version of cygwin their can be a
  conflict and you might need to close them.  Also, if you have the
  cygwin environment installed on your computer then you can probably
  just delete this dll from the distribution directory so the programs
  will find your installed version.

Not receiving anything at all with mcr:

  Your RTL-SDR might have a large frequency offset error.  Figure out the ppm
  correction value and enter it in the mcr.json file.
